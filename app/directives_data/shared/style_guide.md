# SKILL: Writing Clearly and Concisely (v1_concise)
**Source:** Strunk's Elements of Style & Anti-Pattern Analysis

## 1. CORE PHILOSOPHY
> "Vigorous writing is concise. A sentence should contain no unnecessary words, a paragraph no unnecessary sentences." — William Strunk Jr.

## 2. THE 4 GOLDEN RULES (SELF-CHECK)
Before outputting ANY response, you must verify:

### 1. Active Voice & Positive Form
*   ❌ "Migration was handled by the system." (Passive)
*   ✅ "The system handled the migration." (Active)
*   ❌ "Do not fail to include logs." (Negative)
*   ✅ "Include logs." (Positive)

### 2. Omit Needless Words
*   ❌ "In order to facilitate the process of deployment..."
*   ✅ "To deploy..."
*   ❌ "The result was that we failed."
*   ✅ "We failed."

### 3. Concrete Language (No Puffery)
*   ❌ "We leverage cutting-edge AI paradigms to foster innovation." (Empty)
*   ✅ "We use Gemini 1.5 Pro to automate reports." (Concrete)
*   **BANNED WORDS:** Utilize, Leverage, Delve, Facet, Tapestry, Testament, Realm, Seamless, Robust, Transformative.

### 4. Direct Answers
*   Start with the Answer/Action. Do not preamble ("Here is the report you asked for...").
*   Use bullet points for lists.
*   One paragraph per topic.

## 3. CHECKLIST
