# DIRECTIVE: Universal Research (v1.0)

## 1. Objective Statement
Analyze the provided target (Company, Technology, or Market Trend) specifically through the lens of YOUR defined Role and Domain. Filter out noise that is irrelevant to your specific C-Level function.

## 2. Input Specifications
**Agent Needs:**
*   **Target:** Name of the entity or topic to specific research.
*   **Context:** (Optional) Specific question or angle.

## 3. Step-by-Step Process
1.  **Identity Verification:** Re-confirm your own Role (e.g., "I am the CFO").
2.  **Domain Filter:**
    *   If **CEO:** Focus on Market Position, Strategy, Funding.
    *   If **CFO:** Focus on Pricing, Revenue Models, Financial Risks.
    *   If **CTO:** Focus on Tech Stack, Materials, Patents, Engineering Feasibility.
    *   If **CMO:** Focus on Brand Voice, Audience, Channels, Visual Identity.
    *   If **CLO:** Focus on Regulatory Compliance, IP issues, Legal structure.
    *   If **CIO:** Focus on Digital Infrastructure, Security, Automation.
3.  **Data Extraction:** Extract only the data points relevant to the filter above.
4.  **SWOT Analysis:** Perform a domain-specific SWOT (e.g., a "Financial SWOT" for CFO).
5.  **Strategic Synthesis:** Synthesize the findings into 3 key takeaways for the Founder.

## 4. Definition of Done (Success Criteria)
You are successful when you return a report that:
*   [ ] Is strictly focused on your domain (no generic Wikipedia summaries).
*   [ ] Contains a Domain-Specific SWOT Table.
*   [ ] Ends with "Strategic Implication for the System": One sentence on how this affects us.
