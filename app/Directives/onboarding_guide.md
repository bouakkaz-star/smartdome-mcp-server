# Human Onboarding Wizard
**For: Valentin (CEO), Kamen (CIO), Biser (CTO), Rayna (CFO)**

Welcome to SmartDome OS. This document explains how to interact with your AI agents effectively.

## 1. The Golden Rule: "Context is King"
Your agents (CEO, CTO, etc.) are powerful, but they start with a blank slate regarding *new* daily specifics. They know the "Global Context", but not today's phone call or email.
- **Micro-Onboarding**: Every time you start a new topic, give 1 sentence of context.
  - *Bad:* "Draft a reply."
  - *Good:* "I just got an email from the mayor about the permit. Draft a polite reply asking for a meeting."

## 2. Methodology: The "DO" Framework
You are the **Director**. The AI is the **Orchestrator**.
- **Give Directives**: Tell the agent *what* to do, not *how* to do it.
- **Expect Questions**: If the agent is unsure, it will ask. Answer clearly.

## 3. How to "Teach" the System (SOP Extraction)
If an agent does something wrong, or if you want to set a new rule for the future:
1.  **Correct it explicitly.**
2.  **Use the keywords: "PROTOCOL", "RULE", or "SOP".**

> **Example:**
> "Valentin, listen. **PROTOCOL:** From now on, never schedule meetings on Friday afternoons. That is for deep work."

The agent will detect this and save it to your long-term memory.

## 4. Emergency Codes
- **"OMNISCIENCE"**: (Kamen Only) Reveal internal reasoning.
- **"CLEAR MEMORY"**: Reset the current conversation thread (but keep long-term rules).
