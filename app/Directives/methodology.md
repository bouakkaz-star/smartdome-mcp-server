# HAP Model (Human-AI Pairing) Methodology
## For the SmartDome Core Team

***"One Mind, Six Specialized Agents."***

## 1. The Core Philosophy
SmartDome operates as a symbiotic team where each human leader has a dedicated AI counterpart.
*   **Valentin (CEO):** Vision, Strategy, Capital. Paired with Virtual CEO & CMO.
*   **Biser (CTO):** Engineering, R&D, Manufacturing. Paired with Virtual CTO.
*   **Rayna (CFO/CLO):** Finance & Legal. Paired with Virtual CFO & Virtual CLO.
*   **Kamen (CIO/CMO):** Systems, AI, & Marketing Execution. Paired with Virtual CIO & CMO.

## 2. The Directives (SOPs)
**"The Law of the Dome"**
Directives are specific rules captured from the human experts.
*   **Valentin's Directives:** Strategic goals (e.g., "Hvoyna Pilot is priority #1").
*   **Biser's Directives:** Technical constraints (e.g., "Glass concrete must be 20% translucent").
*   **Rayna's Directives:** Fiscal/Legal limits (e.g., "No contracts without CLO review").
*   **Kamen's Directives:** System architecture (e.g., "All data flows via Zep").

## 3. The Workflow (DO Framework)
1.  **Directive (Human):** The human partner issues a goal.
2.  **Orchestration (Team):** Agents coordinate.
3.  **Execution (Agents):** The agent produces the draft/code/document.
4.  **Review (Human):** The specific human partner approves the work.

## 4. The Goal
To create a seamless "Hive Mind" where the AI amplifies the specific expertise of each board member.
